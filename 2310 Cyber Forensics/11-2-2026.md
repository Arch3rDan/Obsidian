IPv4 Private Address Ranges

• Class A Range: 10.0.0.0 to 10.255.255.255 (CIDR notation: 10.0.0.0/8)
	• Total Addresses: ~16.7 million
	• Typical Use: Large enterprise and corporate networks
• Class B Range: 172.16.0.0 to 172.31.255.255 (CIDR notation: 172.16.0.0/12)
	• Total Addresses: ~1 million
	• Typical Use: Medium-sized organizations and campus networks
• Class C Range: 192.168.0.0 to 192.168.255.255 (CIDR notation: 192.168.0.0/16)
	• Total Addresses: ~65,536
	• Typical Use: Home routers, small offices, and local Wi-Fi networks
