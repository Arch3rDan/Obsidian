What rules and laws regulate cyber crime for us and other people

18 USC 1029
	cannot use a computer to commit fraud
	for identity theft cases
18 USE 1030 (Computer fraud and abuse act)
	addresses unauthorized access to computer systems
	addresses illegality of Denial of Service attacks (DoS)
	Anything that could cause damage

ECPA Electronic Communications Privacy Act
	Cannot intercept unauthorized communications though electronic means 
	Applies to networks under a company's control assuming the user has a reasonable expectation of privacy
	this is why your employer will add a disclaimer to your system that there is no reasonable expectation of privacy while using the system

CALEA
	digital telephony act
	complements the ECPA
	Ensure the lawfully authorized surveillance is technically possible at the telecomm provider
	does not require decryption of E2E comms, just an interception point

EEA
	Economic Espionage Act of 1996
